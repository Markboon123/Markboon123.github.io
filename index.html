About

Hi, I'm Mark.

I'm a senior studying Computer Science at Penn State with a minor in Engineering Leadership Development, graduating in May 2027.

This past summer I interned at Accenture in Bangkok as a Business Analyst and Data Engineer, building components of a new Modern Data Platform for Kasikornbank, one of Thailand's largest banks: PySpark ingestion pipelines into Delta Lake, transformation and validation logic, and a bilingual data dictionary for payment and transaction tables. Before that I was a Data Scientist Intern at Business Applications Co., building data pipelines and PyTorch models and presenting the results to executives.

On campus I work on perception for the Advanced Vehicle Team and plan events for the Japanese Student Association. In my spare time I build Top Charge, a browser fighting game.
